The final POC-plan decision remains with the human US Solution Architect. The AI agent performs retrieval, reasoning, drafting, and Delivery handoff preparation, but it does not autonomously approve or reject the plan. The human can accept or reject the draft, and the decision is recorded as an OS event and used as feedback for template ranking. This boundary is intentional because the challenge separates agent volume work from human decision-making while making the human decision part of the learning loop.
